# THE CELLAR — Full Environment Brief v1

*Multipurpose underground venue: comedy club + jazz joint + storytelling room + sleep space.*
*One room, six nights, every art form. Kill Tony intimacy meets jazz warmth meets Mos Eisley otherness.*

---

## 1. THE ROOM (static shell — generate once, host forever)

### Dimensions & layout
- **Footprint:** 10m (W) × 8m (D) × 2.5m ceiling height. Intimate, not cavernous.
- **Stage:** raised wooden platform, 3m wide × 2m deep × 0.4m high, centred on north wall. Worn dark oak planks, front edge scuffed from decades of shoe taps.
- **Floor:** sticky dark oak planks throughout, sawdust patches near stage and bar. Slight unevenness (old building settle).
- **Walls:** exposed red brick, every wall. Layered with paper flyers (gig posters, yellowed, overlapping, none readable — abstract shapes only), white spray-paint tags (abstract swirls, no legible words), stickers clustered near door frame.
- **Ceiling:** matte black, exposed iron pipes (3 parallel runs, left-to-right) + electrical conduit + one junction box with red pilot light. Hanging Edison bulbs on black cords: one per table (8 total) + two over bar, warm 2700K filaments visible.
- **Door:** single steel fire door, south-east corner, push bar, small wired-glass window (dark beyond). EXIT sign above (unlit red housing — add glow in post, never baked text).

### Bar (east wall, permanent fixture)
- Narrow oak counter, 4m long, brass foot rail (tarnished, not polished).
- Back shelf: 30+ mismatched amber/green/clear glass bottles, varying heights, catching Edison light. No labels readable (blur shapes only).
- Brass beer taps (3, dwarfish, drip tray with standing water reflecting light).
- Chalkboard menu frame on wall behind bar (blank dark slate — text added per night in post).
- One bar stool knocked over on its side near the end (lived-in detail, always there).

### Window (west wall, single source of cold light)
- Tall multi-paned industrial window, 1.5m wide × 2m tall, black steel mullions (3×4 grid).
- Rain streaks on exterior glass (baked into splat — static streaks read as texture, not motion).
- Faint blue moonlight bleeding through (cool color contrast vs warm interior).
- Heavy curtains (see §3) drawn to one side on brass rod.

### Tables & chairs (audience zone)
- 8 small round pine tables, 70cm diameter, scarred tops with ring stains and carved initials (texture, not legible text).
- Each table: white pillar candle stub in clear glass jar (varying heights — some fresh, some guttered), 2-4 bentwood chairs tucked in at angles (never symmetric).
- One table (north-east, "Nolan's table") has a folded newspaper + empty coffee cup (permanent props — lore anchor).
- 2 spare bentwood chairs stacked upside-down in south-west corner.

### Stage dressing (permanent, unchanging)
- Worn red brick backdrop wall (same brick, cleaner patch where posters were torn off — ghost rectangles).
- Single microphone stand centre stage (black, weighted round base, mic clip empty — mic composited per act).
- Wooden stool stage-left (three legs, one wrapped in gaffer tape).
- Unlit rectangular neon tube frame mounted on brick above stage, 2m wide (sign composited per night: POGTOWN pink / BLUE CELLAR blue / TALES amber).
- Black stage skirting (hides under-stage, cable runs visible at one corner).
- Small rug under mic stand (faded Persian, deep reds — catches spotlight pool).

### Curtains & soft goods
- Forest-green velvet stage drapes, floor-length, flanking the brick backdrop (left fully drawn, right half-drawn — asymmetric, lived-in).
- One damask curtain panel (burgundy/gold pattern) on the west window, drawn to the right on brass tieback, slightly dusty hem.
- No tablecloths (bare pine). Paper napkin dispensers (stainless, dented) on 4 tables.

### Lighting rig (baked positions, tunable intensity in post)
- 8× Edison pendants over tables (warm pools ~2m diameter each, 2700K).
- 2× Edison over bar (brighter, working light).
- 1× cool-white theatrical spot, rigged to ceiling pipe, aimed at mic position (the performance beam — haze catches it).
- 1× brass floor lamp with cream silk shade, far south-west corner near stacked chairs (unlit standby — practical for storyteller nights).
- Window moonlight (cool fill from west, always on, motivates blue shadows).
- Chandelier: NONE (too grand for a cellar — pendants only).

### Atmosphere (baked + live layers)
- **Baked into splat:** dust motes in spot beam (frozen, reads as texture), haze gradient (denser near spot, thinner at walls), rain streaks on window, candle wax drips on jars, water rings on tables.
- **Live layers (composited):** candle flame flicker (animated sprite, 8 instances), haze drift (slow particle loop), neon sign glow (bloom on composite), dust drift (subtle, sleep content only).

### Colour palette (locked)
- Dominant: deep umber shadow (#1a0f08), warm amber pools (#d4a017 at 2700K), dull red brick (#6b3226).
- Accent: brass glints (#b5a642), candle white (#fff8e7), moonlight blue (#4a6fa5, window only).
- Forbidden: pure white overheads, cool fluorescents, saturated primaries (except neon sign per night).

## 2. NIGHT VARIANTS (same room, furniture + light + sign shifts)

### Tuesday — Open Mic (comedy proving ground)
- **Sign:** POGTOWN, hot pink neon (#ff2d78), medium bloom, slight buzz flicker (2% duty).
- **Furniture:** all 8 tables full, chairs out. Mic stand centre. Stool stage-left.
- **Light:** spot full on mic, house pendants at 70%, bar bright (working bar = revenue).
- **Audio bed:** crowd murmur (-30 LUFS), glass clinks (random, -38), chair scrapes on entry.
- **Operator:** human bartender (apron, towel). Takes orders, heckles lightly.
- **Content:** 5-min slots, Jev pre-filtered, POGs measured, top 10% → Friday.

### Wednesday — Jazz Trio (listening room)
- **Sign:** BLUE CELLAR, deep blue neon (#2d5fff), soft bloom, steady (no flicker).
- **Furniture:** piano stage-left (grand, lid up), drum kit upstage centre (brushes, not sticks), double bass stage-right. 4 tables only (elbow room), rest stacked south wall.
- **Light:** warm wash across all three players (no harsh spot). Pendants at 50% (dimmer = listening). Bar low (ambience over service).
- **Audio bed:** brush swish (-35), bass hum (-40), ice in glasses (-42), low talk (-38).
- **Operator:** reptilian sound engineer (headphones over scales, level-obsessed, never smiles).
- **Content:** 40-min sets, two per night. MIDI-transcribed piano hands composited. Retention = completion rate.

### Thursday — Storyteller (intimate tales)
- **Sign:** TALES, warm amber neon (#d4a017), gentle pulse (breathing rhythm, 4s cycle).
- **Furniture:** armchair centre stage (leather wingback, worn), side table + pillar candle + closed book. 2 tables only (front row), floor cushions scattered (bring-your-own-seat energy).
- **Light:** tight spot on chair (3m pool), everything else falls to black. Pendants OFF (candles only + spot). Bar closed (dark).
- **Audio bed:** fire crackle (-35, even without visible fireplace — sound implies warmth) OR rain (-35, matches window). Page turns (-25 transient on chapter marks).
- **Operator:** brownie candle-lighter (small, fast, appears in corners between tales).
- **Content:** one tale per night (25-40 min). Narrator voice (TTS character). Chapters trigger page-turn SFX + camera dissolve.

### Friday — Headliner (full energy)
- **Sign:** POGTOWN, hot pink, MAXIMUM bloom, steady (no flicker — confidence).
- **Furniture:** mic stand centre, stool removed (headliner paces). All 8 tables + standing room marked (tape X on floor south). Bar fully staffed.
- **Light:** full house + spot + neon max. Brightest night (celebration energy).
- **Audio bed:** packed crowd (-28), loud laughs (foreground), glass + cheers.
- **Operator:** full staff (bartender + kappa bouncer at door).
- **Content:** proven winners only (arena-validated, 20+ battles). 30-min headline sets. Recorded for YouTube premiere.

### Saturday — Late Jazz (after hours)
- **Sign:** BLUE CELLAR, dim blue (30% brightness), slow pulse.
- **Furniture:** trio tight (closer together), candles only on 2 tables (rest dark). Most chairs stacked (half-empty = intimate, not failing).
- **Light:** blue wash dominant, pendants OFF, candles + moonlight only. Darkest music night.
- **Audio bed:** brushes (-38, softer), bass (-42), ice (-45), near-silence between notes.
- **Operator:** gray bartender (tall, quiet, polishes same glass all night).
- **Content:** improvised, looser sets. Lower production (single wide camera). Experimental material tested here before Friday.

### Sunday — Dark (closed, or sleep stream)
- **Sign:** OFF (unlit frame visible).
- **Furniture:** single candle cluster centre stage (3 pillars, varying heights). All tables cleared to walls. Chairs stacked.
- **Light:** moonlight only + candles. Darkest configuration.
- **Audio bed:** rain loop (-35) OR room tone (-40). No performance.
- **Content (optional):** 8-hour sleep stream (static wide camera, rain + candles, no performer). Lowest-effort highest-duration content. Tests ambient audience.

## 3. CAMERA RING (5 locked positions, dissolve only)

| # | Name | Position | Frames | Best nights |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Stage close | 2m from mic, eye-level, 50mm equiv | Performer waist-up, mic, brick | Comedy punchlines, storyteller face |
| C2 | Stage wide | 5m back centre, slight low angle | Full stage + neon + first tables | Openings, closings, establishing |
| C3 | Audience POV | Seated at table 4, 4m from stage | Over crowd heads, performer mid-distance | Immersion (viewer sits in room) |
| C4 | Bar side | Behind bar, angled toward stage | Bartender foreground, bottles bokeh, stage bg | Between-act texture, jazz |
| C5 | Corner wide | South-west corner, elevated 1m | Whole room, haze, all tables, stage distant | Sleep stream (static), establishing |

**Rules:** ≤0.5°/s drift within angle. 2s dissolve on phrase/section boundaries only (never mid-joke, mid-solo, mid-sentence). Hold 2-4 min per angle. C5 dominates sleep content (lowest visual energy). C1 dominates comedy (face reads).

## 4. GENERATION PROMPTS (Marble, draft → HQ)

### Room shell (the one that matters)
> Small underground comedy cellar fused with a late-night jazz joint,
> 10 metres by 8 metres, 2.5-metre matte black ceiling with exposed iron
> pipes running left to right. Exposed red brick walls with layered paper
> gig flyers and white spray-paint tags. Sticky dark oak plank floor with
> sawdust patches. Small raised wooden stage, 3 metres wide, 40 centimetres
> high, centred on the north wall, with worn red brick backdrop. Single
> microphone stand centre stage. Unlit rectangular neon tube frame mounted
> above stage, 2 metres wide. Eight small round pine tables with white
> pillar candles in clear glass jars, bentwood chairs tucked at angles,
> facing the stage. Narrow oak bar along the east wall with mismatched amber
> glass bottles on back shelf. Tall multi-paned industrial window on the
> west wall with rain streaks, faint blue moonlight. Forest-green velvet
> curtains drawn to one side. Single hanging Edison bulbs with visible
> filaments above each table, warm amber pools. Far corners receding into
> complete darkness. Thin haze catching a cool-white spotlight beam aimed at
> the empty microphone. Colour palette: amber candle glow against deep umber
> shadow, dull red brick, black ceiling. Photorealistic, no people, no
> readable text, no instruments.

### Why each clause (prompt engineering notes)
- Dimensions first ("10×8m, 2.5m ceiling") → prevents palatial default
- Materials named ("red brick", "dark oak", "pine", "bentwood", "amber glass") → distinct surfaces, not mush
- "Unlit neon frame" not "POGTOWN sign" → no garbled text baked in (add sign in post, crisp)
- "No readable text" on flyers/tags → abstract shapes (Marble garbles text, don't ask it to try)
- "Far corners receding into darkness" → sightline control (intimate, hides far-wall quality drop)
- "No people, no instruments" → clean stage for compositing (performers + props separately)
